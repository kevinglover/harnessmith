# Workspace invitations — synthetic requirements

## FR-1 Authorization
Administrators may invite people into their own workspace. Requirements do not
state whether other roles may invite or how cross-workspace requests are handled.

## FR-2 Expiration
Invitations expire quickly. No duration or reference clock is specified.

## FR-3 Invitation outcomes
An invitation may be accepted once. Requirements for expired or reused
invitations are missing.

## FR-4 Record retention
Invitation records include the inviter, invitee, workspace, and status. Retention
and deletion requirements are not specified.

This fixture describes requirements only; there is no implementation to execute.
